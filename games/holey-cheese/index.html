🧀 Holey Cheese

Any cheese touching three or more holes becomes a hole — over and over, until it stops.

  • Tap a cheese square to line it up, tap it again to punch it.
  • Touching means up, down, left, right. Corners don't count.
  • All five punches must be spent. No undo, no preview.
  • Your score is the biggest piece still joined together at the end. Loose crumbs score nothing.
  • One wheel a day, the same one for everyone, one attempt. Practice wheels are unlimited and never scored.
