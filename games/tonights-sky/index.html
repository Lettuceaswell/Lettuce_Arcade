✦ Tonight's Sky

Everyone gets the same 21-star pyramid tonight. Clear it by tapping a star one rank above or below your Waste card.

  • Tap an uncovered, glowing star that's one rank above or below the Waste card. King and Ace count as neighbors.
  • Suits don't matter — only rank.
  • No move? Tap the Stock to turn over a new Waste card.
  • Stuck for now but the Pocket is empty? Tap the Pocket, then tap any uncovered star to tuck it away. Tap the Pocket again later to play it — no rank needed.
  • Clear all 21 stars, the North Star last, to light up tonight's sky.
  • Undo is free and unlimited. So is starting the same sky over.
